FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

If it's not answered here, the answer is usually 'it's computed from your data'.

Do you use sample or fake data?

No. Ledgerly runs entirely on the file you upload — profiling, analyses, insights, dashboards and presentations all compute from your exact data. There is no hidden sample workspace.

Is this a real ML pipeline?

Not yet, and it never pretends to be. Every result is computed by deterministic in-repo logic, and anything prototype-grade carries an explicit Prototype / Demo badge. When live pipelines ship, the labels come off.

Where does my data go?

Your choice. In the browser build, DuckDB-wasm and Pyodide do the compute client-side. On the server, queries run on a worker thread and data lives in your deployment's database — SQLite locally, PostgreSQL in production. The app is self-hostable.

Which databases can I connect to?

PostgreSQL, Snowflake and BigQuery connectors are built in, with scheduled sync. Everything else — and everything in a hurry — starts as a CSV or XLSX upload into DuckDB.

Can I export my work?

Yes. Analyses and notebooks export as Python or .ipynb, reports as HTML, and charts as SVG. Nothing you produce is trapped inside the product.

What exactly does the prototype label mean?

It means the result was produced by deterministic prototype logic rather than a live production pipeline. It's a promise: the product will always tell you when a number is demo-grade.
